Hi, I'm Kevin.

I'm a DevOps engineer who loves tinkering with tech, hardware, and open source. This blog is my space to share projects, experiments, and the occasional scambaiting adventure.

You'll find posts about:

* **Hardware hacking** – I've built a networked standing desk controller, decoded i2c on consumer devices, and learned C++ by sniffing data from my desk.
* **Linux adventures** – From experimenting with window managers to switching to NixOS for the love of learning.
* **Software tinkering** – I've written everything from kernel modules to shell scripts, and I'm always curious about how things work under the hood.
* **Scambaiting** – Sometimes I like to waste the time of online scammers. It's harmless fun that helps keep real victims safe.

I believe in the hacker ethos of learning for learning's sake. Sometimes the best projects are the ones with no practical purpose other than the joy of exploration.

Feel free to reach out on [Mastodon](https://fosstodon.org/@kn100) if you'd like to chat about tech, cybiko, or life.
